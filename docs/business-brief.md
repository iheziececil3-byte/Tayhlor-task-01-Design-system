# Business Brief — D Williams Fashion Hub

## The Business

D Williams Fashion Hub is a tailoring and fashion business serving customers from children to adults. Williams consults customers about their preferences to help determine suitable designs. Customers choose fabric quality according to their budgets.

## People Who Use the Application

**Customers.** Adults aged 18 to 75, ordering for themselves or, as parents and guardians, for a child. Williams makes clothes for all ages, so accounts are intended to be held by adults, with a child's orders under a parent's or guardian's account.

Most customers are assumed to use smartphones on mobile data of variable quality, though some will use a laptop or computer. Technical confidence varies widely, so the interface must not depend on users knowing tailoring or web-app jargon.

**Tailor.** Williams needs fast access to customer and order information without slowing production.

*Age range, devices, connection quality and technical confidence are working assumptions, not measured data. The interview confirmed who the clothes are for and the tailor's concern about production delays.*

## The One Thing That Must Work

Allow a customer to submit a clothing order, receive the tailor’s acceptance or rejection and proposed completion timeframe, and proceed to payment only after the tailor accepts the order.
## Three Recurring Questions

1. **“Can you be able to do it?”** Customers want to know whether Williams can make their requested garment or design. Answers must reflect his verified capabilities.
2. **“Can you design this job for me?”** Customers want to know whether Williams can help create their requested outfit. Answers must reflect his actual design services and consultation process.
3. **“When will it be ready for collection and fitting?”** Customers want to know when their particular order will be ready. The answer must come from reliable order information, not a guess.

Williams reported these questions during the interview. They are not unique to his business only, so answers must reflect his verified business information and each customer's own order records.

## Intended Interface Feel and Why

The interface should feel **elegant, rich, and refined**. Fashion is about appearance and personal expression: people dress how they want to be addressed. The visual design should communicate style, confidence, and quality while keeping information easy to understand and everyday tasks practical. Richness should come from deliberate typography, imagery, spacing, and restrained visual choices—not unnecessary decoration that makes the application harder to use.
